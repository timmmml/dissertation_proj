understand how vocalisation is used for social feats

	- *core result: dialects per family (characteristic vocal usage patterns - is there a common backbone? if so is it genetically determined?)!*

	- deep learning-based system to do sound source localization. Training by moving a speaker around the environment and predict coord+interval.  
	- speaker dataset published as a baseline
- how does the brain encode/decode these signals for behavior?
	- speaker playback of calltypes --> see vocal response, (singing mice do these too)
	- robust behavior
	- what ongoing population dynamics in auditory cortex are going on?
