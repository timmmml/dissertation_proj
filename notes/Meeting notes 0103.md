Goal: 
1: uncover representations with minimal contraint
- investigate properties of neural representations over manifolds by training continuous attractors that maintain reps in those manifolds with minimal assumptions.
- analyze effects of hyperparameters (outlining constraints a bio circuit might face) to the resulting representations, in terms of measurable tuning curves. 

2: linking representations to behavior by studying goal-directed motor control. 
- 

All these goals work toward a key *utility*: evaluating this framework as a basis of linking data to hypothesis. 
- with the CAN framework and explicit network setup, we have access to loads of stuff: 
	- neural activity patterns and population codes
	- connectome
	- *dynamics*
- using these as a baseline, can use clustering-based algorithms for discovering the hypothetical variable (could be one of several candidate manifolds, or one of several control schemes), given data in these realms (mapping, update, or connectome)
- provide a prior to latent dynamical systems without inductive biases.
