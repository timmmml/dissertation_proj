### The Hopfield Model

Basic setup

- two states in the model system:

$$
V \in \{ 0, 1 \}^{N}
$$
- states transition by the thresholding function

$$
V_{i} \to \text{sign}( \sum_{j} W_{ij} V_{j} - U_{i} ) ==1
$$
- asynchronous compuation and strong back-coupling (compared to the perceptron)

Whe information storage algorithm: 

to store set of patterns $\{ V^{s} \}_{s \in [1, n]}$: 

$$
\begin{align}
W_{ij}  & = \sum_{s} (2V^{s}_{i} - 1)(2V_{j}^{s} - 1), \quad W_{ii} = 0 \\
\sum_{j}W_{ij}V_{j}^{s'}  & = \sum_{s}(2V_{i}^{s} - 1)\left[ \sum_{j} V_{j}^{s'}(2V_{j}^{s} - 1)\right] = H^{s'} _{j}
\end{align}
$$

let's do matrix form so it's easier to parse

$$
\begin{align}
V_{t + 1}  & = \mathrm{sign}(WV_{t}), \quad U = 0\\
WV^{s'}  & = \sum_{s} (2V^{s} - 1)(2V^{s} - 1)^{\top}V^{s'} = H^{s'}\\
 & = \sum_{s}(2V^{s} - 1)(2V^{s\top}V^{s'} - 1^{\top}V^{s'})
\end{align}
$$

comment: $W \in \{ -1, 1 \}^{N \times N}$
essentially for each pattern, compute a similarity metric between the network state and the pattern, and add up the similarities. 
This way, the most similar will eventually dominate. 

Lyapunov energy account: 
$$
\begin{align}
E  & = -\frac{1}{2}V^{\top}WV\\
\Delta E  & = -\Delta V^{\top}WV
\end{align}
$$
So energy monotonically decreases into stable states .

Three types of behavior in random (not necessarily symmetric weights) networks:  
- fall into a few discrete attractors
- limit cycle across a few states
- chaotic behavior
 (within short Hamming distance from one particular state)
