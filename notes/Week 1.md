#### Guillaume's remarks
if you take SO(3), you could imagine representing the upcoming rotation using the standard 3x3 matrix formulation, i.e. 9 numbers tied together in some specific way, but it's unclear how neural _activity_ at the go cue might represent such matrices (e.g. we tend to think of neural activity as vectors, not matrices, and the sort of network dynamics that are required to effectively perform matrix-matrix multiplication to compose rotations is completely unknow as far as I am aware)  
alternatively, you could imagine representing a rotation using a quaternion (4 numbers, which you could embed in neural activity), but again just how RNN dynamics could implement quaternion multiplication (this operator obeys very specific rules) is also unknown  
our network might come up with neither, and that would be super interesting, too
