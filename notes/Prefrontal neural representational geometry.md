# Experimental paradigm

- recorded from lPFC in (n = 6) monkeys, dealing with a DMTS (delayed match-to-sample) task, whose stimulus is either object location or object identity. 

- The monkeys are trained to keep a cue (*cue 1*) within its working memory and indicate if it is the same with a second stimulus (*cue 2*). Delay periods separates the presentation of *cue 1*, presentation of *cue 2*, and the choice stage. 

- Pre- and post-training data are recorded - single unit recordings over time across trial unfolding are available. 

# Main results

Method
- **PCA**-based dimensionality reduction of neural actiivty over time. Measured geometrics between these representations (*primary angle*) across conditions such as the training status (pre- vs. post-training). 

- In the case of the spatial task, no training is required to retrieve from the first PCA components a geometry like that implied by the task setup (ring-like across stimuli, corresponding to their locations).
