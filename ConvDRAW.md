Source: [Towards Conceptual Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.08772) by Gregor et al. (2016)
NeurIPS 2016

Result: 
- ConvDraw that can transform an image into a *progression of increasingly detailed reprsentations*: global conceptual aspects to fine-grained details. 
- insights: 
	- positioning multiple layers of stochastic variables close to pixels to increase generative performance
	- stratification of information helps with high-quality lossy compression.
